Plotline · SystemEarth
SystemEarth challenge · multi-layer, multi-year geospatial data

Forest layers reduced to what the plot decision reads

EUDR screening intersects farm plots with years of forest maps, and repeats the check whenever a map updates. Plotline stores only the facts that check reads: a decision layer 99% smaller than the original layers, with identical answers for all 744,732 map cells and 44× faster re-checks of registered plots.
