Deep dive

What the plots show: declarations and mapped observations

Five of the 17 supplied plots carry a supplier declaration of an "indication of deforestation". Hansen and JRC map post-2020 loss on different plots. Two variants (an older Hansen release, a different cell-inclusion rule) do not reconcile the two, and the comparison does not establish the cause.

In brief
  • Five plots carry a declared indication, recorded mitigation with evidence, and a compliant final status: P-0003, P-0005, P-0008, P-0010, P-0013. These are declarations; the evidence files were not supplied.
  • Hansen alone maps loss after 2020 on 7 of 17 plots. With JRC's 2020 forest map, only P-0001 has mapped loss on mapped 2020 forest: 2 cells in 2022. Region-wide, 341 of 80,429 post-2020 loss cells (0.4%) are on mapped 2020 forest.
  • The declared plots and the mapped plots differ, and neither an older Hansen release nor a different boundary rule reconciles them. This is a question for the reviewer; the mapped observations are not compliance findings.
1 · What suppliers declared

Five plots declare an indication, then a compliant status.

The supplier's record covers 17 plots (plot IDs only are used here). For five, the record states that an "indication of deforestation" was found using Hansen Tree Cover Loss 2021–2023, that on-site verification with evidence was recorded, and that the final status is "Deforestation-free" and "Compliant" for EUDR.

This pattern is a review lead. It does not establish a violation, an error in the record or a legal status. The evidence references in the record are opaque keys, not links, so the mitigation itself is not visible.

3 · What the decision layer adds

Almost all post-2020 loss is on land JRC does not map as 2020 forest.

EUDR asks about forest on 31 December 2020, not about any tree loss. Hansen loss also records harvests on land that was not forest in 2020. The decision layer therefore combines Hansen with JRC's 2020 forest class, and counts only mapped loss on mapped 2020 forest.

Why those cells are not forest in the JRC map is not established; a plantation harvest cycle is one possibility. The wording used is "falls outside JRC's mapped 2020 forest class", nothing stronger.

ScopeThese are mapped observations from public layers under a written rule. They are not compliance findings.
Detail: why one layer alone overstates post-2020 loss technical

On the region, 42.9% of land cells show Hansen loss at some point since 2001 (319,011 of 743,418). A Hansen-only forest proxy (canopy above 10%, no loss to 2020) marks 58.4% of land as forest and counts 71,030 post-2020 loss cells on it; JRC marks 15.9% as forest. That gap is why a rubber and oil-palm mosaic needs the second layer. At JRC's native ~9 m resolution, P-0001 has 554 forest cells out of 1,083 JRC cells.

The JRC rule reads one JRC cell per Hansen cell (the centre). Reading a 3×3 majority or "any forest in the block" instead changes no plot decision, but only P-0001 has forest, so it is a weak test. "Any forest" moves the regional total of loss-on-forest cells from 341 to 506 and P-0001's from 2 to 4.

5 · Variants tested

Neither an older release nor a different rule reconciles the declarations with the maps.

Two candidate explanations were tested: the supplier used an older Hansen release, or assigned cells to plots by a different rule. The method was fixed before the results were examined. In every variant, none of the five declared indications is reproduced in the 2021–23 window.

These tests cannot establish what the supplier did: manual review, a threshold, another layer or a buffer are all possible. A plot-level decision can be checked only if the layers, versions and rules behind it can be replayed; Plotline's receipts record these.

6 · Known zero and unknown

"No loss seen" and "could not be read" are different answers.

Every Plotline answer carries one of four truth states. Missing data is never reported as zero.

The grid is invented. In the app, unknown is drawn hatched and never as 0.

Known zero

Every selected cell was read and none shows loss. Shown as "none" with complete support.

Positive

At least one loss cell was seen. It stays positive even if other cells are missing: missing data cannot cancel loss that was seen.

Unknown

Some cells could not be read, and none of the rest shows loss. The answer cannot be "no loss", only "none seen".

Unavailable

Nothing can be computed: no cell centres, no valid land, or a required layer is missing.

7 · Limits

Limits of the comparison

  • Cells, not hectares. Every count is a number of ~28 m map cells.
  • The 0.5 ha minimum area and the "able to reach 5 m" test from the EUDR forest definition are not applied by any layer here. "Deforestation" here means mapped tree-cover loss on mapped 2020 forest.
  • Hansen loss includes fire and logging and does not say what the land became. The cut-off is checked at year resolution.
  • Protected areas are not included, because their licence is unclear.
  • One region, one laptop. The 34 × 17 km sample region is the only area measured. JRC is read at the Hansen cell centre, a documented simplification.
  • The supplier's release, rule and method are unknown. A current Hansen release may differ from the one they used.
  • Source and compact agreeing shows the representation keeps the answer under the written rule. It does not show the maps are right, nor that anyone complied.
8 · Open questions

Open questions for review

A small next step is one plot and one receipt: marking which fields answer the real review question, where the decision rules differ, and how an unresolved evidence reference is handled. A corrected query contract would add more than another dataset.

  • Question 1 · The reference queryWhat exact historical layer release, spatial inclusion rule, canopy and forest definitions and missing-data rules produced the supplied results?
  • Question 2 · The evidenceWhat do the opaque reference keys resolve to? Can one representative mitigation or evidence item and the expected review steps be shared?
  • Question 3 · The workloadIs the common operation one interactive plot, a batch of thousands, repeated fixed-cohort checks, or arbitrary regions after layer updates? The workload should pick the representation.
  • Also to confirmWhether the supplied sample may appear in published material, and the commodity and region of interest beyond this sample.

Company-reviewed cases are best kept separate from the 17-record test set, and supplier final declarations are not independent ground truth for training a classifier.
