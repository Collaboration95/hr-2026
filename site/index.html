Plotline: a decision layer for EUDR plot checks

Plotline reduces four forest-map layers (Hansen canopy, loss year and land mask, plus JRC 2020 forest) to one 20-code decision layer per map cell, and answers plot-level screening queries from it. The sample region's 623,237 B of original layers become a 6,146 B store with identical answers for all 744,732 cells.
