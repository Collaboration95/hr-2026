Explainer pack

Keep the decision, not the data.

SystemEarth asked how to store years of satellite forest maps compactly, query them fast, and still give every farm plot the same deforestation answer. Plotline's decision layer is 99% smaller, re-checks registered plots 44× faster, and gives the same answer for all 744,732 map cells.
