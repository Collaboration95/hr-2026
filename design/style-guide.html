Plotline · design system · v2 · 15 Oct 2026

Night survey

A dark, map-first instrument. Panels sit quietly around a luminous raster. Colour is reserved for meaning: every data concept owns one hue, and none is reused for anything else. Spec: design/DESIGN.md. Tokens: app/src/styles/tokens.css.

01 · Colour

One hue per meaning

Lilac only ever means declared by the supplier. Ember only ever means mapped tree-cover loss. Lime means proof / exact match. Amber is a question for a human, never a violation. Unknown is always hatched.
