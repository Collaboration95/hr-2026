## Limits that matter

Only three heldout macroregions in one source tile are independent spatial clusters. Millions of cells do not create millions of independent validation cases. Validation contains no mixed/coastal patches, while holdout contains five; the joint selector loses substantially on that regime. Descriptive three-cluster resampling intervals do not establish population confidence. All source validity masks in this study happen to be true; broader mask support relies on codec tests, not this cohort. Sparse patch packages cover listed patches only, not continuous regional support.

The18heldout patches are spent. A revised coastal policy would need new development coverage and a fresh test cohort. There is no demonstrated operational need to undertake that additional ML work during this lab: strong fixed standard controls already remove the apparent gain. Rejecting the selector is the evidence-backed optimization decision.
