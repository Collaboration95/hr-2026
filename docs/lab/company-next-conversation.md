## The useful problem we can demonstrate

An analyst receives plot boundaries and due-diligence declarations. They need to inspect the mapped observations, understand missing support, and explain the basis for each result. Meanwhile, the engineering team needs to choose a geospatial representation that preserves the same answer with defensible storage, query and resource tradeoffs.

Plotline connects those tasks: a linked evidence index, plot/year/native-cell inspection, declaration references, original-versus-compact proof and portable receipts. Its implemented screening rule is explicitly provisional. Agreement proves preservation of that declared computation; it does not prove source accuracy, supplier honesty, mitigation sufficiency or legal compliance.
